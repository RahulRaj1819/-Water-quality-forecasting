💊 Recommended Treatment Methods

General purification techniques recommended for making unsafe water potable.

🔥
Boiling
Heat water to a rolling boil for at least 1 minute (3 min at high altitudes) to kill bacteria, viruses, and parasites.
💧
Reverse Osmosis (RO)
Forces water through a semi-permeable membrane removing up to 99% of dissolved salts, heavy metals, and organic contaminants.
🔦
UV Purification
Ultraviolet light at 254 nm wavelength destroys the DNA of microorganisms, effectively disinfecting without adding chemicals.
🪨
Activated Carbon Filter
Adsorbs chlorine, organic compounds, pesticides, and unpleasant odors/tastes through porous carbon media.
⚗️
Chlorination
Adding controlled amounts of chlorine (0.2–0.5 mg/L residual) kills harmful pathogens. Widely used in municipal water treatment.
🔄
Ion Exchange
Replaces harmful ions (e.g. heavy metals, nitrates) with safe ions using specialized resin beads. Excellent for water softening.
🏗️
Sedimentation
Allows suspended particles to settle by gravity. Often combined with coagulation (alum/ferric chloride) for turbid water pre-treatment.
